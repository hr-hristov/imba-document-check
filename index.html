Document check · design document

Catch the contradiction before the client does

imba is an app for running AI agents. Each agent works in a chat, called a spawn, inside one folder of files. Most documents a business delivers live in such a folder: a final report with its budget and annexes, a tender bid, an annual report, a policy set, next to the contract or brief they must agree with.

Several agents write on one set of documents at the same time, each in its own spawn and its own part. Each agent sees only its own changes, so the documents drift apart: one agent shortens the delivery period in the report while another still prices the old one in the budget. Document check is a new command that reads every page, finds statements that cannot both be true, fixes what the reference documents settle, and checks every document again in rounds until one comes back clean.

Final reportpage 18

“The complete portal, including data migration and staff training, was delivered within eight months of signing the contract.”

Budgetpage 2

“The amounts above cover ten months of implementation work, followed by the warranty period.”

Both can go to the client today. Only one can be true.

Any set of documents that goes out

The check reads prose, not code, and it works on any set of business documents that must agree with each other before delivery. A few of the sets it is meant for, each with the kind of clash it catches:

Client deliverablesThe final report says the work took eight months. Its budget covers ten.
Tender bidsThe technical proposal names nine key experts. The price offer pays for seven.
Annual and board reportsThe chair's letter says revenue grew 12 percent. The accounts show 9.
Proposals and statements of workThe proposal includes staff training. The statement of work leaves it out.
Due diligence and audit reportsThe summary calls a lease expired. The annex lists it as running to 2028.
Policies and proceduresThe policy asks for two signatures on a payment. The procedure asks for one.
Grant applicationsThe work plan runs 24 months. The budget pays the staff for 18.
Contracts and their annexesThe contract says payment within 30 days. The payment annex says 45.

The documents the set must agree with, such as the contract, the statement of work, the client's brief or terms of reference, are its reference documents. They decide which side of a clash is wrong.

What was asked

A check like imba's code review, which sends several read-only AI reviewers over a change and confirms what they find. This one reads business documents instead of code.

Business documents to be deliveredReports, proposals, bids, contracts, plans, policies and CVs, written by several agents at once. Prose, in Word and PDF.
Every statementEach checkable statement is pulled out with its quote and page, then compared with every other statement on the same subject.
Several reviewersSeven read-only reviewers in each round, and every subject is compared by two of them.
Guaranteed to finish the workimba checks each step itself before the next starts. A skipped page or a silent reviewer stops the round.
Rounds until cleanThe check repeats until a round finds nothing new, up to a round limit the operator sets.
The clash on the cardThe result card quotes the two statements. Six looks for it are drawn below, at phone and desktop width.

How one round runs

A round has six steps. After each step imba checks the work itself, in code, before the next step starts. A reviewer's word that it finished is never taken alone.

  1. 0

    Choose the documents before round 1

    Every Word, PDF, OpenDocument, markdown and text file in the spawn's folder and its subfolders. Word's lock files and the Skip list are left out. Over the document limit of 60, the run stops and says how many it found, rather than checking part of them.

    imba checksA PDF with no text in it, such as a scan, is named on the card as unreadable. Nothing is skipped without saying so.
  2. 1

    Read imba

    imba turns each document into text once, before any reviewer starts. Every line carries its document, page and section heading, so Claude and Codex reviewers read the same words.

    imba checksEvery page of every document was read.
  3. 2

    List the statements 2 readers

    The documents are split into two even batches. Each reader lists every checkable statement in its batch: the sentence word for word, its page, the claim in one plain line, and its subject, such as “warranty” or “delivery period”.

    imba checksEvery quote is found on its page. A quote that is not there is dropped.
  4. 3

    Compare 3 hunters

    Statements that share a subject are bundled, and each bundle goes to two of the three hunters. A hunter calls each pair agree, clash or not comparable, looking for six things: figures, dates, duties, scope, names and logic.

    imba checksEvery subject came back from two hunters.
  5. 4

    Check against the references 2 checkers

    Checkers test every statement against the reference documents and answer holds, wrong or cannot tell, citing the page that decides. They also redo every total and percentage, and every figure written out in words.

    imba checksEvery statement has an answer. imba adds up each sum itself, so a model's arithmetic never decides alone.
  6. 5

    Confirm the spawn's agent

    The agent reads both passages in place and looks for a reading under which both are true, such as another lot, another phase or another currency. Each clash is kept with its kind, or dropped with the reason.

    imba checksEvery clash has a written reason, kept or dropped.
  7. 6

    Fix the spawn's agent

    Where a reference document or a redone sum shows which side is wrong, the agent rewrites that side in the document's own words. A clash nothing settles is never edited. It goes to the operator with both statements and a recommended answer.

    imba checksEvery change is found in its file afterwards.

A reviewer that gives no answer, or answers in the wrong shape, is run again. A round is never counted with a gap in it.

Rounds until clean

A fix can start a new clash: correcting the total cost on page 31 can break a sum on page 33. So after any fix, the next round reads every document again from the start, not only the changed pages. The run ends when a round finds nothing new, or when the round limit is reached.

3 rounds by default

The round limit is the operator's: any number from 1 to 20 in Settings, or for one run with /rd rounds 6.

Clean after 2 rounds

A whole round found nothing new, and nothing waits on the operator.

1 decision left

Nothing new in the last round, but a clash the reference documents cannot settle waits on the operator.

Not clean after 3 rounds

The last allowed round still found something. The card shows what and on which pages.

Each clash carries one word for how sure the run is. imba works the word out from the records; the agent never chooses it.

proven

A reference document or a redone sum shows one side wrong.

sure

Both hunters found it and the agent confirmed it.

likely

One hunter found it and the agent confirmed it. Dropped when Keep one-hunter finds is off.

Starting a run

The operator types /rd, short for review documents, in a spawn working in the documents' folder. Words after it change that one run only; the saved settings stay as they are.

What gets fixed

Automatic fix is on by default, and only for clashes a reference document or a redone sum settles. Every fix is checked by the next full round like any other statement.

  • Word filesA tracked change by “imba”, which the document owner accepts or rejects in Word. imba refuses the change when the old words are missing from the page or appear on it twice.
  • Markdown and textEdited in place.
  • PDFNever edited. The new wording goes into the fix plan.
  • A clash nothing settlesNever edited. Both statements go to the operator with the agent's recommended answer.
